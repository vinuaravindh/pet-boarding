About Me

Hi, I'm Sangeetha, a pet lover based in Sharjah. Pet's have always been a big part of my life since childhood, and I love every moment of it. As a stay-at-home mom, I've faced the struggle of finding a reliable pet sitter myself, so I decided to step in and help others who need the same. My family and I adore having furry friends around, and my kids especially enjoy showering them with hugs and cuddles. Reach out today to see how I can help your pet feel at home!

Frequently Asked Questions

What pets do you take care of?

Dogs, cats, rabbits, tortoises, and other small pets — including senior pets and pets with special dietary needs.

Will my pet be caged or kenneled?

No. Every pet stays in my home as part of the family, with room to roam — not in a cage or kennel.

Will I get updates while I'm away?

Yes. You'll get daily photos and videos throughout your pet's stay, so you always know how they're doing.

Do I need to meet you before booking?

Yes. We start with a meet-and-greet so your pet can get comfortable with me and my home before their stay, and so we can discuss any special requirements.

Where are you located?

Al Majaz 3, Sharjah, UAE.

How do I book a stay?

Reach out on WhatsApp to discuss your pet's needs and schedule, then we'll set up a meet-and-greet and lock in your dates.
